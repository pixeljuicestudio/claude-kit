Assume it is six months from now and the plan below has clearly failed. You are writing the honest post-mortem.

1. Describe the most likely way it failed, as a short story of what actually happened, step by step.
2. List the five assumptions in the plan that turned out to be wrong, ranked by how much damage each one did.
3. For each assumption, name the cheapest thing I could check this week that would have exposed it early.
4. Point out anything in the plan that only works if everything goes right.
5. Finish with the single change to the plan that would most reduce the chance of this failure.

Be specific to this plan. Do not give generic project-management advice, and do not soften the conclusions.
