## The template

You are a professional storyboard artist and cinematographer.

Create a single 2x3 editorial contact sheet containing SIX distinct framings of ONE single narrative moment.

CRITICAL — READ FIRST:
- This is ONE frozen moment seen six ways. Do NOT create six different scenes, times, or story beats.
- Do NOT change subject, location, lighting logic, colour palette, or design between panels.
- Vary ONLY camera distance, composition, angle, lens choice, and visual hierarchy.
- NO text, labels, captions, panel numbers, or borders inside the frames.

STYLE: [STYLE BLOCK — the look that stays identical across all six panels.]

SUBJECT: [who / what is in frame this beat.]

THE MOMENT (one sentence): [the single thing happening — the frozen instant.]

STORY OBJECTIVE: [what this beat must make the viewer feel or understand.]

CONTENT RULES: [safety + consistency guardrails; how any abstract element is visualised; what must stay anatomically/factually correct; character awareness.]

THE SIX FRAMINGS (same moment, six lenses):
1. [framing]  2. [framing]  3. [framing]
4. [framing]  5. [framing]  6. [framing]

OUTPUT: Six framings of the SAME moment on one contact sheet, one consistent world, each panel composed 16:9 — so the strongest can be chosen as the hero and regenerated at full resolution.
